Project Overview

Vcare Opticals provides comprehensive eye-care services, including routine exams, contact lens fitting, and advanced diagnostic screening. This website refresh will present the clinic as modern, approachable, and easy to navigate.

Goal

Help every patient achieve clear, comfortable vision through personalised service and the latest optometry technology, increase local appointment bookings by making contact easier, and strengthen community confidence by highlighting patient-centred care and transparent pricing in every part of the site.

Success Evaluation

  • Increase overall contact form submissions by at least 25% within the first three months after launch, demonstrating improved user confidence and conversion from visitors to leads.
  • Achieve a measurable increase in click-through rate from the home page to the contact/booking page, proving that calls-to-action are clear and navigation is intuitive.
  • Reduce the average time it takes for new users to locate booking and contact options to under 45 seconds, which will validate enhanced usability and efficient information architecture.

Target Audience

  • Community residents aged 25–65 who require annual or biennial eye exams, contact lens fittings, and preventive care.
  • Parents of children aged 6–17 seeking reliable pediatric eye-health screening and kid-friendly exam procedures.
  • People with diabetes, hypertension, and other chronic conditions requiring specialist retinal and glaucoma screenings every 6–12 months.

Design Approach

  • Use clear, large text and simple navigation labels (Home, Services, About, Contact) with prominent booking buttons so busy adults (25–65) can quickly find and schedule appointments.
  • Include dedicated pediatric service highlights, friendly imagery, and parental reassurance content for families seeking child screenings, differentiating from general wellness sites that are less family-specific.
  • Feature a specialist section for chronic-condition management, highlighting diabetes-friendly screening protocols and regular follow-ups, ensuring this high-risk group finds dedicated clinical pathways not usually emphasized on broad optometry websites.

Key Site Content

  • Home — Clinic overview, key services, and a clear call-to-action to book an exam.
  • Services — Detailed descriptions of exams, contact lens fitting, dry-eye treatment, and diagnostic screening.
  • About — Introduce the team (Dr. Melissa Javali, Dr. Alan Koya, Sarah Ravie, Emiliano Brooklin) and their expertise.
  • Contact — Location, hours, phone, email, and a booking/contact form.
  • Announcements — Highlight promotions like first-exam discounts and special offers.

Site Structure

Navigation will be consistent across all pages: Home → Services → About → Contact. Key actions (bookings, contact) will always be easy to locate.
